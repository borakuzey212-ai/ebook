Questions

Before you buy

What do I get after paying?

A download link by email, usually within a minute. The PDF opens on any phone, tablet or computer and prints cleanly, logbook pages included.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every program is a one-time payment. You keep the file for good, and you receive free updates whenever that edition is revised.

Which program should I start with?

New to training, with access to a barbell: Strength Foundations. No equipment at all: Home Workout Fundamentals. Coming back after a long break, or stiff from desk work: run Mobility Reset first, then pick a program.

Do I need a gym?

Not for most of the catalog. Home Workout Fundamentals, Bodyweight Mastery, Dumbbell Only, Kettlebell Conditioning and Mobility Reset all run at home. Every product page lists its equipment before you pay.

Can I get a refund?

Digital files can't be returned, but if a program isn't what you expected, email within 14 days and we'll refund it. We'd rather you trust your next purchase.
